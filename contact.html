A few quick answers.

Can I ask for a shoe that isn't shown in the catalogue?

Yes. The online catalogue is only a selection. If you cannot find the style you're looking for, contact the MK team on WhatsApp and tell us what you need.

Do you deliver outside Masindi?

Yes. MK Shoes Center offers deliveries across Uganda. Contact the team with your location so we can discuss the available delivery option.

Do you offer installment purchases?

Yes. Installment purchases are available. Contact MK directly for the current arrangement and requirements.

How do I ask about a product?

The quickest option is WhatsApp. Send the product you're interested in or describe what you're looking for and the MK team can assist.

Do the products have prices on the website?

Prices are intentionally not displayed in the catalogue. Contact the MK team for current availability and product details.
