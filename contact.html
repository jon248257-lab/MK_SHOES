Before you message us

Questions?
Start here.

We've kept the important answers here so you can quickly understand how MK Shoes Center works before placing an enquiry.

01

Where is MK Shoes Center located?

We are located in Kijura, Masindi, near Kisalabwire Round About and Round About Academy.

02

What time are you open?

MK Shoes Center is open Monday to Sunday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

03

Do you deliver outside Masindi?

Yes. MK Shoes Center provides deliveries across Uganda. Contact the team on WhatsApp to discuss your destination and order.

04

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Installment purchases are available. Message the MK team directly so they can explain the available arrangement for the product you want.

05

Why can't I see every shoe on the website?

The online catalogue is only a selection. MK Shoes Center has more products than the website can display, so if you cannot find what you want, contact the team.

06

Do products have prices displayed online?

Prices are not displayed on the website. Contact MK Shoes Center directly through WhatsApp for the current information about the product you are interested in.

07

How do I order a shoe?

Find the product in the catalogue, tap the WhatsApp option, and send your enquiry. You can also message the team directly if you already know what you want.

08

Can I send a picture of a shoe I want?

Absolutely. If you have seen a shoe elsewhere or have a particular style in mind, send the picture through WhatsApp and ask the MK team about it.
