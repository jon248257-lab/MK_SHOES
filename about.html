01 / The beginning

A local idea
with a bigger

MK Shoes Center exists for people who understand that footwear is not simply something you put on your feet.

It is part of how you present yourself, how you move, how you work, how you celebrate and how you express your personality. From everyday essentials to statement styles, we curate footwear for real people with different lifestyles and different stories.

Our home is Kijura, Masindi — but our ambition has never been limited by a single street, town or district. We are building a footwear experience that feels local at heart and confidently connected to the wider world.
